Privacy Policy for PDF Bnao

Effective Date: September 27, 2026
App Name: PDF Bnao – Image to PDF
Developer: Keshav Studios
Contact: keshavstudiosindia@gmail.com

Keshav Studios ("we", "us", or "our") operates the PDF Bnao – Image to PDF mobile application (the "App").

We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how information is handled when you use PDF Bnao.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, what information may be collected by third-party services, how information is used, and your choices.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

PDF Bnao is designed so that your documents and images can be processed directly on your device.

We do not require you to create an account to use the core functionality of the App.

We do not collect or upload the following through the App's core document-processing functionality:

  • Photos selected from your device
  • Images imported into the App
  • Documents scanned using the camera
  • PDF files created by you
  • Pages, scans, or document contents
  • Text contained in your documents
  • Camera images used for scanning

Your selected images and scanned documents are processed locally on your device for features such as editing, cropping, rotating, filtering, reordering, and PDF creation.

We do not sell your documents, photos, or document contents.

2. Camera and Photos

PDF Bnao may request access to your device camera when you choose to scan a document.

Camera access is used to:

  • Capture document images
  • Detect document edges
  • Capture multiple pages
  • Create scanned PDF documents

Camera images are processed for the scanning functionality and are not uploaded to Keshav Studios' servers as part of the App's normal document-processing functionality.

The App may also allow you to select images from your device using Android's image/file selection functionality.

We only access images or files that you choose to provide to the App.

3. Files and Documents

PDF Bnao may create and store PDF files on your device when you use the App.

These files may include:

  • PDFs created from images
  • Scanned documents
  • Edited pages
  • Images selected by you for PDF creation

Files created by the App are stored locally on your device or in locations selected by you through Android's file/storage functionality.

We do not intentionally upload your created PDFs or document contents to our servers.

4. Advertising and Google AdMob

PDF Bnao displays banner advertisements using Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google.

AdMob may collect or process certain information from your device to provide, measure, secure, and improve advertising services.

Depending on your device, location, consent choices, and Google's advertising configuration, this may include information such as:

  • Advertising ID
  • IP address
  • Device and app information
  • Approximate location derived from IP address or other available signals
  • Information about ad interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Information necessary to prevent fraud and abuse
  • Advertising and measurement information

The information collected by Google through AdMob is handled according to Google's applicable policies and terms.

AdMob does not receive the document images, scanned pages, or PDF contents processed by PDF Bnao merely because you use the document-scanning and PDF-creation features.

Google may use information collected through its advertising services for purposes such as advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, security, and personalization where permitted by applicable law and your consent choices.

5. Personalized Advertising and Consent

Depending on your location and applicable privacy requirements, Google may request your consent before using certain information for personalized advertising.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Google requires publishers using AdMob to use an appropriate consent mechanism for applicable advertising purposes.

Where required, PDF Bnao will provide the appropriate consent or privacy choices through Google's approved consent mechanisms.

Your advertising choices may affect the type of advertisements displayed to you.

PDF Bnao currently uses third-party services that may process certain information necessary to provide their services.

Google AdMob

AdMob is used to display banner advertisements in the App. Google may process device, advertising, diagnostic, and advertising-interaction information as described above.

7. Internet Access

Important: PDF Bnao's core document-processing functionality is designed to work on-device and does not require you to upload your documents to a cloud service. However, the App may require internet connectivity for third-party services such as advertising.

Therefore, while document creation and editing can operate locally, certain features such as loading advertisements may require an internet connection.

8. Data Sharing

Keshav Studios does not sell your personal information or document contents.

We may share or permit access to information with third-party service providers when necessary for services integrated into the App.

In particular, Google AdMob may collect and process advertising-related information as described above.

We do not intentionally send your scanned documents, PDF files, photos, document text, or camera scans to advertising providers for advertising purposes.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App.

Document processing is performed locally on your device whenever supported by the App's functionality.

However, no software, device, internet transmission, or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should use appropriate device security measures such as a device lock, encryption, and keeping your operating system up to date.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Keshav Studios does not maintain a server-side database containing your scanned documents or PDFs as part of the App's normal document-processing functionality.

Documents and PDFs created by PDF Bnao may remain on your device until you delete them.

You can delete documents created by the App using the App's document-management functionality or by deleting the corresponding files from your device.

Because advertising services are provided by Google, certain advertising-related information may be retained by Google according to Google's policies and applicable legal requirements.

11. Children's Privacy

PDF Bnao is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through the App.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at keshavstudiosindia@gmail.com.
